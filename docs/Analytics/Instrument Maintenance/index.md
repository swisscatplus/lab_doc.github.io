## HPLC-DAD-ELSD-FC-MS / HPLC-DAD-MS-Prep / SFC-DAD-MS(iQ) / SFC-DAD-MS(IM-QTOF)

* Once a week: Washing the SQ with IPA/H2O (50:50) with a special paper for

* Once a month: Washing the SQ following these steps
1. Disassemble the source and the closure !Be careful with the inlet capillary, there's an invisible needle -> put a tips on this part to avoid breakages!
2. Put all parts in a beaker !Except for the nebulizer that you have to put in a graduated cylinder!
3. Add hot water and put it in the ultrasonic bath for 5 min
4. Put milli-q water and do the same process as before (thrice)
5. Put IPA and do the same once
6. Put MeOH and do it once as well
7. Dry the parts (N2 or with the paper for)
8. Wash your gloves with water to prevent contaminations onto the source
9. Reassemble the source

* Sometimes need to empty the waste containers -> alarms are there to detect when containers are full

## NMR

* Once a week: Filling the magnet with N2:
1. Take the Deware to the filling station
2. Make sure that the outlet pipe is open (on)
3. Open the filling station panel and turn on with the key
4. Connect the filling station pipe to the inlet part of the Deware and open this section (turn on)
5. Click "start" on panel -> the filling takes approximatively 30min 
6. Once the Deware is full you can press on "stop" and turn off with the key
7. Turn off the inlet part of Deware
8. Put off the filling station pipe
