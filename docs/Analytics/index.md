# Analytical platform
## Structure
The analytical platform of the laboratory includes multiples instruments and is devided into 4 parts:

1. **Sreening step**
- 2 Analytical HPLC - DAD - MS (SQ) - Fraction collector (FC)/ELSD
- 1 SFC - DAD - MS (SQ) - ELSD
- 1 GC - MS (EI)
2. **Sample preparation**
- Bravo
- Centrifuge
- Labeler
- Evaporator
3. **Characterization step**
- 1 Semi Preparative HPLC - DAD - MS (SQ)
- 1 Multicells HR - UV
- 1 FTIR
- 1 NMR 300MHz
- 1 SFC - DAD - IM - QTOF
4. **Autoplant**
- 1 Semi Preparative HPLC - DAD - MS (SQ) - Fraction collector(FC) open bench


All chromatography machine and HR-UV are purchased from Agilent. The NMR and FTIR are bought from Brucker.
