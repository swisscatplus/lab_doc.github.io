# High-Pressure Reactive Gases – CatScreen and Autoplant

This procedure describes the use of **H₂** on the **CatScreen** as an example. The steps are, in principle, also applicable to **other reactive gases** and the **AutoPlant**.

# ⚠️ Pressure Safety Notice

<span style="color:red; font-weight:bold;">
IMPORTANT: The maximum pressure allowed for both systems is **80 bar**.
</span>

Exceeding this limit may cause **serious equipment damage** or **safety hazards**.  
Always verify pressure settings before starting an experiment.
