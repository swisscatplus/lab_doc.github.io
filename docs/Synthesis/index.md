# Synthesis Platforms  

At **SWISSCAT**, three **Chemspeed platforms** are employed for homogeneous catalysis research and process optimization.  
These systems are operated under a controlled **nitrogen atmosphere** to maintain inert conditions throughout all experimental workflows.  

---

## Swing SP and CatScreen – Discovery  

The **Swing SP** and **CatScreen – Discovery** platforms are dedicated to **High-Throughput Experimentation (HTE)** and **screening studies**.  
They are designed for experiments in **Paradox plates** accommodating **48 to 96 samples** in **1–2 mL vials**:  

- [48-position Paradox plate](https://www.analytical-sales.com/product-category/photoredox-parallel-synthesis/aluminum-reaction-blocks/hplc-vials-2ml-12x32mm/hplc-48-position-photoredox-4-bolt/)  
- [96-position Paradox plate](https://www.analytical-sales.com/product/standard-96-position-photoredox-lightweight-reaction-block-gen-ii/)  

**Purpose:**  
These systems enable **parallel catalytic experiments** on a large number of samples, with the goal of identifying the **optimal reaction parameters** to maximize yield or **improve enantiomeric excess (ee)**.  
All experimental data are collected by the Chemspeed software and subsequently integrated into **AI-based predictive models**. This accelerates catalyst discovery by reducing the number of experimental iterations required to identify the most effective catalyst.  

---

## AutoPlant – Optimization  

The **AutoPlant** platform is focused on **reaction optimization and kinetics**.  
It employs **240 mL reactors** for kinetic investigations. Following an initial screening campaign, the most promising catalyst or condition can be scaled up to **200 mL** in **three PD (Process Development) reactors**.  

The reactors are fully integrated with **bench-top NMR** and **FT-IR spectrometers**, enabling real-time **reaction monitoring** and **kinetic analysis**.  
They can be operated either under **inert atmosphere** or **pressurized with reactive gases**, depending on the experimental requirements.
